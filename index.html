01 · About

I turn measurements from space into knowledge we can trust: where a spacecraft is, where it's pointing, and what it sees.

I am an aerospace engineer based in Riyadh. I hold an M.S.E. in Aerospace Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin and worked at UT's Center for Space Research on lunar geodesy and reference frames. Since 2025 I have worked in industry on satellite communications, navigation and Earth-observation systems.

My work spans three connected areas of space engineering. What ties them together is the same habit: model the system, estimate it from real or simulated measurements, then test the result hard enough to know how far it can be trusted, whether that means validating against a reference implementation, running a Monte Carlo, or checking a covariance against the actual error.

03 · Projects

Selected projects

Graduate projects from UT Austin. Click any figure to enlarge it.

Orbit determination

JahSat-1: EKF/UKF orbit determination

Estimated a satellite's trajectory from simulated range and range-rate data at three stations (Kwajalein, Diego Garcia, Arecibo). Built the filters in stages, from two-body dynamics with ideal measurements up to J2 and realistic station geometry, and tuned process noise with small Monte Carlo runs. Compared EKF and UKF across scenario sweeps (range-only, range-rate-only, per-station) over six days plus a seventh-day prediction, judging them by residuals and by whether the covariance matched the actual RIC error.

EKF / UKFResidualsRIC errorsBias estimation

Outcome: quantified filter performance by measurement mix and geometry. This project is where my interest in covariance realism started.

Orbit design & control

Replicating TerraSAR-X orbit control

Recreated TerraSAR-X's 11-day sun-synchronous repeat orbit and control strategy. Built truth and reference orbits with J2 and drag, designed ΔV maneuvers to stay within a 250 m tolerance tube, and explored maneuver cadence against error growth and propellant.

Orbit propagationSun-sync / repeatControl budgetMATLAB / Simulink

Outcome: demonstrated control feasibility under perturbations; outlined extensions for SRP and higher-fidelity drag.

Attitude determination & control

Closed-loop ADCS with an MEKF

Built an ADCS simulation: rigid-body attitude dynamics with environmental torques, an Earth-pointing PD controller, actuators with error models, a multi-sensor attitude suite, and a six-state multiplicative EKF for attitude and gyro-bias estimation. Robustness evaluated with a 100-run Monte Carlo.

MEKFPD controlSensor fusionMonte Carlo

Outcome: bias convergence and pointing accuracy across disturbance cases; sensitivity to sensor noise quantified.

Radar remote sensing

SAR image reconstruction

Implemented time-domain back-projection focusing from raw complex data, with range compression, azimuth focusing and multi-looking, to produce high-resolution images that are clearly sharper than unfocused methods.

SARBack-projectionSignal processingImaging

Outcome: focused imagery of urban scenes with a visible resolution improvement over the unfocused baseline.

Earth observation · ML

EO object detection: tree crowns

Benchmarked Faster R-CNN, RetinaNet and SSD for tree-crown detection, integrated DeepForest as a baseline, and prepared a dataset protocol for a fair comparison.

DetectionCNNPyTorch

Outcome: a standardized pipeline for precision/recall benchmarking on EO imagery.
